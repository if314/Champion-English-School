About Champion School

An English school for students in grades 2 to 12

Champion School provides specialized English language education in Plovdiv for students at primary, lower-secondary and upper-secondary level — from grade 2 through grade 12. We work in groups clearly organized by grade and level, so every student can progress at the right pace.

Our goal is simple: students should leave lessons with real confidence to speak, write and understand English — not just memorize grammar for a grade.

Why Champion School

Why families choose Champion School

Groups by grade and level

By grade

Courses from grade 2 to grade 12

We group students into three core stages, aligned with the Bulgarian school curriculum.

Grades 2-4

Primary stage — first steps in English through play, songs and simple conversation, building a foundation and confidence.

Grades 5-7

Lower-secondary stage — expanding vocabulary and grammar, with emphasis on reading, writing and speaking.

Grades 8-12

Upper-secondary stage — deeper grammar, academic vocabulary, and confidence for school assessments, school-leaving exams, and future study or work that requires English.

Method

How we teach English at Champion School

1

A level test before you start

Every new student takes a level test to join a group that matches their knowledge.

2

Small groups

We keep groups small so every student gets the chance to speak and receive feedback.

3

A balance of grammar and speaking

We combine structured grammar and vocabulary work with practice in real conversational situations.

4

Tracking progress

Students and parents receive regular feedback on how language skills are developing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which grades is Champion School suitable for?

Champion School offers English courses for students in grades 2 through 12, grouped by grade and by English level.

How is a student's level determined?

The level is determined with a free online level test, available on the “Level Test” page, before choosing a suitable group.

How does the level test work?

The test has short multiple-choice questions and takes only a few minutes. At the end you get an approximate result for the student's English level.

How do I choose the right group?

After the level test we recommend a group based on grade and result. We can discuss and adjust the choice further during enrollment.

Where are the classes held?

Classes are held in Plovdiv, at 145 Shesti Septemvri Blvd.

How can I enroll a student?

Enrollment is done through the form on the “Enrollment” page or by phone at 0885 712 048. We will contact you to confirm the group and schedule.

What is the groups' schedule?

Group days and times are confirmed once each group is formed for the current school year. The general structure by grade and level is shown on the “Schedule & Prices” page.

What are the prices?

Prices depend on the group, level and course duration. Up-to-date information is available on the “Schedule & Prices” page or by contacting us.
